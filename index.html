Three complementary branches

Summaries below follow the branches' README files. They describe different responsibilities, not three competing scientific conclusions.

01 / BROAD DISCOVERY

Dimensions Beyond Energy

The concept-discovery and source-gathering layer searches across energy systems, materials, intelligence, frontier physics, and transformations that reduce civilization's energy needs. Its Phase-1 atlas includes 90 candidates and 81 annotated sources. Dimension G separately explores governance and transition risks if a pathway succeeds.
