# DBE Concept Atlas - Phase 1

**Dimensions Beyond Energy**  
*The Search for a Civilization Beyond Energy Scarcity*  
Research snapshot: 2026-10-04

> What physically permissible discovery, technology, combination of technologies, or transformation of civilization could make energy cease to be a binding constraint on intelligent life?

## Scope and reading guide

This research-first collection contains **90 candidate concepts**, anchored to **81 annotated source records**, with **19 software repositories** and **11 discovery/data portals** in the companion library. The material began as a research snapshot and is now preserved in this branch as the first DBE concept-atlas intake. No simulations or experimental reproductions were performed as part of the source-gathering phase.

Every candidate has source anchors, but an anchor can be a background reference or a component experiment rather than proof of the proposed application. **19 candidates are explicitly marked background-only or adjacent-source coverage.** See `SEARCH_AND_GAPS.md` for that list. Some remaining candidates also have incomplete mechanism-to-application evidence. The atlas is not a comprehensive or systematic literature review.

The D/P/S/X labels below are **Phase-1 discovery tags**, not the canonical SDBES claim-evaluation system and not definitive scientific consensus. They are retained because they are useful for broad intake, but formal assessment must occur at the **Claim** and **Claim Assessment** level under the SDBES typed evidence framework. In particular, evidence quality, evidence direction, formal validity, empirical support, engineering demonstration, probability, dependency, causal influence, and structural importance must not be collapsed into one number. See the `SDBES` branch documents `docs/SDBES/V1_TYPED_EVIDENCE_DEPENDENCY_LEDGER.md` and `docs/SDBES/V2_CLAIM_EVALUATION_RULES.md` before treating any Phase-1 tag as a scientific conclusion.

## Branch A: Nuclear energy and fusion architectures

### DBE-A01 - Compact high-field tokamak power

**Class:** P - Physics-compatible frontier. **Role:** generation.

**Bounded question:** Can a compact high-field tokamak export dependable net electricity?

**Mechanism status:** Fusion and high-field magnets demonstrated separately

**Energy/resource ledger:** Fusion fuel; heating, current drive, magnets, cooling and fuel-cycle loads must be included.

**Main gap:** Integrated wall loading, fuel sufficiency, exhaust, availability and cost.

**Next discriminating investigation:** Construct a whole-plant power ledger with uncertainty and an experimentally anchored confinement model.

**Source anchors:** [N01: The SPARC Toroidal Field Model Coil Program](https://doi.org/10.1109/TASC.2023.3332613) (2024); [N08: Laying the Foundation for New and Advanced Nuclear Reactors in the United States](https://www.nationalacademies.org/read/26630) (2023); [N13: Plasma Physics and Fusion Energy](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/plasma-physics-and-fusion-energy/CD7B530D2889F70446F34E14EE0EF703) (2007).

**Coverage:** Direct component evidence plus systems background. **Formal confidence:** Not yet independently assessed.

**Possible software:** [TORAX](https://github.com/google-deepmind/torax); [PlasmaPy](https://github.com/PlasmaPy/PlasmaPy); [OpenMC](https://github.com/openmc-dev/openmc); [OpenMDAO](https://github.com/OpenMDAO/OpenMDAO). These are leads, not tested implementations of this concept.

**Tags:** fusion, tokamak, REBCO, net-electricity.
