# DBE Source and Resource Library

Research snapshot: 2026-10-04. **81 scientific, methodological, historical and official source records; 19 software repositories; 11 discovery/data portals.**

Annotations are original summaries of the relevance and limits of each source, not full-text reproductions. Publication type and screening depth are recorded. Creator fields using "et al." or "Authors listed at source" are deliberately abbreviated and must be expanded from publisher metadata for a submission-ready bibliography. DOIs are included only where identified. Undated live pages retain an access date rather than an invented publication year.

## Historical and foundational reading paths

Read these as foundations or historical proposals, not current performance forecasts. The annotations above retain the depth of screening.

**Energy and magnetism:** C01, the Feynman field-energy chapter; then N13, Freidberg's plasma and fusion text. These provide the language for distinguishing stored field energy, transported energy and a replenishing source.

**Thermodynamic constraints:** E01 on passivity; D01 on an experimental Landauer test; E02 on driven vacuum photon production. The point is to distinguish a conditional impossibility from an effect powered by a specified external resource.

**Extreme gravitational reservoirs:** E11, Blandford and Znajek (1977), alongside E12, Hawking (1975). Their different mechanisms must not be presented as interchangeable or as laboratory power devices.

**Alternative nuclear ideas:** N09, CERN's 1995 energy-amplifier design; N10, the historical muon-fusion review; N14, Rider's thesis as a lead requiring full-text and counterargument review.

**Digital civilization:** D12, the 2008 whole-brain-emulation roadmap, followed by D06-D08. The distinction between a roadmap, a simulation, a wiring map, a consciousness theory and personal-identity preservation remains essential.
