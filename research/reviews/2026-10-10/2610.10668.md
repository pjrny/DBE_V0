CORE: Topologically ordered superfluids are described as phases that keep deconfined, gapped anyons while staying gapless because of Goldstone modes. Gauging the charge symmetry maps such a superfluid to a gapped symmetry-enriched topological order with vanishing Hall conductance, and gauging back recovers the superfluid. Vortices inherit the topological properties of the residual flux defects. Parton examples include non-Abelian vortices in bosonic systems.
