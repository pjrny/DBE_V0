CORE: Gate insertion replaces an involutory gate such as CZ or CX with 2j+1 copies to amplify noise, and that scaling is said to fail for non-commuting gate noise. Heisenberg-echo symmetrization adds an echo that cancels the leading insertion error. In the author's simulations the absolute fidelity error drops by one to two orders of magnitude relative to gate insertion. For pure initial states, high-rank observables can raise the echo variance; rank-one observables do not add sampling overhead relative to gate insertion.
