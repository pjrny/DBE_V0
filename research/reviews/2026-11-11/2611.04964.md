CORE: Asymptotic rate and relative distance are improved for codes with fully addressable transversal phase gates at every fixed level h at least 3 of the Clifford hierarchy, using a cyclic encoding outside binary CSS. A polynomial-time algebraic-geometric construction gives a common lower bound of 52/(675h) on rate and relative distance. A counting argument improves that bound by a factor of about 2.86, at exponential verification cost. Inside the same framework an upper bound sits about 1.28 times above that improved lower bound. Explicit codes with 48 and 90 physical qubits support fully addressable transversal T gates.
