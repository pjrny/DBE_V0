CORE: Coherent Z rotations on the triangular-lattice surface code, followed by syndrome measurements, are related by Wegner duality to a Z2 paramagnet under strongly symmetric decoherence. The authors describe a transition into a partially decodable critical phase and a dual algebraic strong-to-weak symmetry-breaking phase, analyzed with a weakly coupled SO(n) nonlinear sigma model in the replica limit n to 1. Large-scale numerics are reported to match the predicted decoding fidelity and a dual power-law fidelity correlator.
