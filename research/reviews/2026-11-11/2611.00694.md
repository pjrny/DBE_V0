CORE: Photon-loss thresholds are estimated for the surface-GKP code. For finite-energy GKP states with epsilon 0.01 and 0.05, which the authors identify with 20 dB and 13 dB of squeezing, nearest-lattice-point inner decoding plus surface-code matching gives threshold estimates gamma-star of 5.5% (0.24 dB) and 5.9% (0.26 dB). An anti-photon-loss inner decoder that compensates quadrature contraction raises those estimates to 9.8% (0.45 dB) and 6.3% (0.28 dB).
