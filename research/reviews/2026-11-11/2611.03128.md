CORE: Belief-propagation guidance is added to the Tesseract decoder for quantum LDPC codes. If the BP hard decision reproduces the triggered detectors, the Tesseract search is skipped. Otherwise BP marginals, weighted by a trust coefficient, change the search priority while the path cost keeps the channel priors. On the [[72,12,6]], [[90,8,10]], and [[144,12,12]] bivariate bicycle codes under circuit-level noise, BP-based initialization is reported to cut the number of expanded search nodes by more than two orders of magnitude at low physical error rates, with the logical error rate staying close to Tesseract.
