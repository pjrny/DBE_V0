CORE: The paper gives a criterion for decoupling a translation-invariant p-ary CSS code, p prime, into a stack of higher-form toric codes by a finite-depth local CSS-preserving Clifford circuit of SUM and SWAP gates, after ancillas and a superlattice coarse-graining. In higher dimensions the remaining obstruction is gluing between excitation layers. For a code with m nonzero layers, once translations act trivially on every layer, coarse-graining by p to the m-1 in each direction removes that gluing. The same criterion is when the code encodes the maximal number of logical qudits on a finite torus.
