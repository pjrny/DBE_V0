CORE: Memory experiments of the [[144,12,12]] bivariate bicycle code are studied on two shuttling grids intended for future spin-qubit devices: a square grid and a square grid with diagonals. Three shuttling strategies and a range of shuttling-noise models are compared. The best strategy minimizes the longest distance travelled rather than the total distance. Adding diagonals is reported to reduce the logical error rate by up to two orders of magnitude.
