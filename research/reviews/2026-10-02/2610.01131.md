CORE: A quasi-symmetric stellarator can be matched to a simpler tokamak when you only ask about slow particle drifts. This paper asks the same question for turbulence. To lowest order the match is a circular tokamak fixed by aspect ratio, twist, and magnetic shear. Gyrokinetic runs of the ion-temperature-gradient mode agree quantitatively for some configurations. Not every stellarator has such a twin once you ask for the next order.
