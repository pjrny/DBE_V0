CORE: The Richtmyer-Meshkov instability of a single-mode density interface is simulated in an ideal ion-electron plasma with a five-moment multi-fluid model, for thermal, species, and isotope interfaces. Unlike a single-fluid model at matched Atwood number, the three interfaces diverge. Thermal and species interfaces grow harder and develop secondary instabilities. The isotope interface stays closer to the single-fluid response because the electron density ratio across it is unity. Self-generated fields, and the extra growth, shrink as that electron density ratio drops. Stronger ion-electron coupling, read as a smaller plasma skin depth, makes the three cases more alike. The abstract names inertial confinement fusion as one application.
