CORE: Three-dimensional hybrid-kinetic simulations compare freely decaying balanced and imbalanced Alfvenic turbulence, with the same initial amplitude and spectrum and different normalized cross-helicity. The balanced run dissipates more energy and heats protons more efficiently. At sub-proton scales both runs are mostly oblique and right-hand polarized. Only the imbalanced run shows substantial parallel power with ion-cyclotron waves, born where temperature anisotropy grows during the initial steepening, together with parallel proton beams. Non-Maxwellian features in velocity space differ between the two regimes.
