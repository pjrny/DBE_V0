CORE: The paper proposes resonant x-ray absorption imaging with an x-ray free-electron laser as an active probe of dopant mix and burn in inertial-confinement shells. Charge-state imaging of the dopant is meant to constrain ionization, opacity, and mix from hydrodynamic instabilities at stagnation. Proof-of-principle shots probe a hot spot in coated copper wires driven by an ultra-short relativistic laser. Atomic and radiation-hydrodynamic simulations with synthetic images extend the idea to direct-drive shells with copper-doped ablators and indirect-drive shells with tungsten-doped ablators. The abstract says the combination could measure fusion-relevant hot dense plasma at multi-keV temperatures and benchmark radiation hydrodynamics.
