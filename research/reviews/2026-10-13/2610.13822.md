CORE: A Gaussian-process surrogate learns burn-weighted diagnostic states from design variables so those states can be used at prediction time without another simulation. On 1,987 paired one-dimensional implosions, states learned from calculations without alpha-particle heating cut the mean squared log-response error of the global predictor by 60.25 percent on a random holdout and by 52.98 percent on design-space extrapolation. Random projections and shuffled states do not reproduce the gain. Experts help at smaller training sets and add little at 1,000 CH designs. A further 3,000 Be/Cocktail designs show weaker extrapolation and cases where experts hurt. The abstract points to an open Python implementation and to archived data and code.
