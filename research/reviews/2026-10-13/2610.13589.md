CORE: Clifford deformations change the stabilizers of a code to cut the logical error rate without adding qubits, and the best deformation depends on both the noise and the decoder. Evolution-Based Best-arm Identification treats the circuit, the noise, and the decoder as a black box. On the rotated surface code with PyMatching and two strongly Z-biased circuit-level models, the abstract says the search finds the optimal deformation at distance 3 with up to about 1100 times fewer shots than random search. At distance 5 the median search reaches the deformations the authors believe are optimal, and random search does not. Under a Z-biased SI1000-like model they report a new pattern, B-XZZX, which differs from XZZX on a fraction of order 1/d of the qubits, with a relative advantage that grows from about 5 percent at distance 3 to about 12 percent at distance 11.
