CORE: For a non-degenerate braided fusion category, the paper studies the Grothendieck ring of its module categories. A convolution of etale algebras in the Drinfeld center matches the full center of a relative tensor product. The ring is graded by localizations. Each stratum is described by double-coset blocks and a transition matrix, with a sandwich multiplication formula. When the category is a center, the top stratum's transition matrix is the Gram matrix of the Lagrangian algebras.
