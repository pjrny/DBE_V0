CORE: SpeCyl gains a free-boundary module for resistive wall modes and external kinks. Two coaxial thin resistive shells surround the plasma, vacuum is assumed between and outside them, and their positions and time constants can be varied. The linear growth rates of the (m=2, n=1) resistive wall mode and external kink are checked against Shafranov's analytic theory for a circular large-aspect-ratio tokamak with a flat current profile, over a range of wall positions, shell time constants, and reference equilibria. The same growth rates are also compared with earlier resistive-wall modelling used on RFX-mod tokamak plasmas.
