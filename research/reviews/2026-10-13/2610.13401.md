CORE: An Abrikosov-fermion parton theory with random-phase approximation is used for the finite-temperature dynamical spin structure factor of extended spin-1/2 Kitaev models. For the pure Kitaev model it reproduces a strong nearly flat low-energy peak, a broad high-energy continuum, and a downward shift of low-energy weight with temperature, in qualitative agreement with continuous-time quantum Monte Carlo. The continuum shrinks because bond amplitudes fall and the two-parton phase space compresses. Ferromagnetic and antiferromagnetic Heisenberg additions move the low-energy peak at the Gamma point in opposite directions. The ferromagnetic Kitaev-Gamma model softens at finite momentum toward the period-three order seen in other numerics. In a [111] field, a broad parton continuum sits with magnon-like branches.
