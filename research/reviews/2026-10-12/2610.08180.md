CORE: Global gyrokinetic simulations of a KSTAR fast-ion-regulated enhancement mode compare profile relaxation with and without fast ions, at five initial main-ion temperature gradients. With fast ions the zonal-flow and shearing-rate amplitudes are larger and the shear layers persist. At the experimental reference profile those layers sit with temperature-gradient corrugations that the authors call consistent with E cross B staircases. Inward and outward ion heat fluxes then coexist, so the average heat flux is smaller than in the run without fast ions. With fast ions the heat flux falls as the ion-temperature gradient rises, opposite the usual Fick trend. The authors read that as a possible bifurcation rather than a smooth extension of L-mode.
