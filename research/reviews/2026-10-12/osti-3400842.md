CORE: The paper takes a first step on multigrid for the tokamak MHD models in M3D-C1. The hard piece is the implicit momentum advance. The production solver is a block-Jacobi preconditioner inside a Krylov method, with blocks on planes of constant toroidal angle, and that preconditioner degrades as the number of planes grows. The authors build a semi-coarsening geometric multigrid in the toroidal direction on the partly field-aligned grid. They report competitive performance on a runaway-electron model of a SPARC disruption, and better robustness on a stellarator case where block Jacobi does not converge.
