CORE: The paper extends an oscillation-guided belief-propagation decoder with syndrome-flip trials (BP-SF) by windowed and streaming updates, and implements it in CUDA. On the [[144,12,12]] bivariate bicycle code at physical error rate 0.002, parallel BP-SF cuts the 99.9th-percentile call latency by a factor of 13.15 against an eight-chain Relay-BP GPU baseline, on 100,000 matched samples where the first BP stage of every tested method fails inside 80 iterations. Windowed BP-SF is reported to hold a flat logical error rate and a nearly constant per-round time through 512 rounds, with a 17.1-fold speedup against monolithic decoding. In a 512-round full-frame stream with syndrome arrivals every 2.97 ms, layered BP-SF with 20 retries on a transformed joint graph cuts the 99.9th-percentile correction-release age from 166.29 ms to 36.25 ms against flooding updates with 100 retries.
