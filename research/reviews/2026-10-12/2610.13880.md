CORE: Two-block group-algebra codes are treated as a searchable family of quantum LDPC codes, many of them translation invariant on a twisted torus. A census of about 10 to the 8 two-block codes with up to 320 qubits, at check weights five through nine, supports a few exhaustive statements: up to equivalence the Gross code is the only abelian weight-six two-block code with n at most 200 that reaches kd squared over n equal to 12, and the known [[64,18,8]] is the best two-block code by that figure for n at most 94 across those weights. Codes found include [[102,20,10]] and [[120,24,10]] at weight nine, which are simulated in noisy syndrome-extraction circuits, and [[288,16,16]] as the best weight-six two-block code with n at most 300. The abstract says the best codes at weights seven to nine beat the best published exact-distance kd squared over n at the same weight in most length bands up to 320. The codes are in a public database.
