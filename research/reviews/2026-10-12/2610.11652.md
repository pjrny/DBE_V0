CORE: The paper derives the triangular prism equations for near-group fusion rules of type G plus the order of G, with G a finite abelian group. Spherical categorification with trivial Frobenius-Schur indicator, over a field where the group order is invertible, is equivalent to solving that system. For every cyclic group they build a solution with hyperbolic gamma functions: one spherical category with negative dimensions, and a Galois conjugate that is pseudo-unitary with positive dimensions. Scalar solutions with positive dimensions get a singular-value formula and a gap that are sufficient for unitarity. Unitarity of the cyclic Galois-conjugated coefficients for every order is tied to a real-multiplication conjecture that follows from the order-one abelian Stark conjecture.
