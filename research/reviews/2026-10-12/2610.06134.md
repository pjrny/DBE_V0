CORE: The paper proves that energy moments propagate for the three-dimensional plasma-charge model, the repulsive Vlasov-Poisson system with a moving point charge. For every K greater than 2, a finite Kth energy moment stays finite on every bounded time interval, with no higher-moment assumption, no small-mass assumption, and no initial gap from the charge. The global weak solution that is constructed conserves total energy for almost every time. The argument uses a critical space-time density estimate and control of the electric field along the charge.
