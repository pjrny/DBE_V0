CORE: Sub-gigahertz charge driving of superconducting Kerr oscillators in the transmon regime is used both to turn on parametric interactions and to reduce sensitivity to low-frequency charge noise. One oscillator is reset in 82 ns to a residual population below 0.7 percent, under its 2.5 percent steady-state thermal population. Two oscillators encode a dual-rail qubit with nearly fourfold erasure bias. With one end-of-circuit erasure check the error per Clifford is 5.6 times 10^-4, and 1.5 times 10^-4 after post-selection, with 25 ns gates.
