CORE: A dynamic compass code is run on 156 superconducting qubits in a heavy-hex lattice, using the whole monolithic processor and dropping weak components from the measurement schedule. A noise-informed decoder, exclusion of the worst qubits and couplers, and post-selection on low leakage are reported to improve logical error probabilities by up to 35 percent in a memory experiment, and the same strategies by up to 56 percent in stability experiments for measurement-based logic. Lattice surgery is demonstrated. Pre-selection is reported to give Bell fidelities above 77 percent, and logical-gap post-selection with exclusive decoding above 97 percent.
