CORE: Fault-equivalent rewrites are searched so that a fault-tolerant circuit keeps its fault tolerance while Bell-pair reductions remove ancilla preparations and CNOTs. For Shor-style syndrome extraction on the [[7,1,3]] code, one round is reduced from 30 to 18 ancilla preparations and from 54 to 42 CNOT gates. At a physical two-qubit error rate of 10^-3 the optimized circuit is reported to lower the logical error rate by about 21 percent for both logical basis states, with the reduction between 13 and 23 percent over two orders of magnitude in p. A Steane-style search selects four ancillas and 14 CNOT gates and is reported to lower the logical error by about 15 percent under depolarizing idle noise at 3p/10, and by about 10 percent at p/10.
