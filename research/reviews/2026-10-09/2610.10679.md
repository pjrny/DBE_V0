CORE: Gapped superconductors in 2+1 dimensions with condensate charge 2ne are classified by how their vortices intertwine with topological order. The top of that list is called SC-sharp: the elementary vortex of flux h/(2ne) cannot be written as an invertible-superconductor vortex dressed by bulk anyons, and every elementary vortex is non-Abelian. For that family the elementary vortex sector is given quantum dimension at least sqrt(6) and is said to be unable to satisfy Fibonacci fusion rules. The phases are built by anyon condensation.
