CORE: The paper builds quantum secret sharing from CSS qLDPC codes and computes which coalitions are authorized. It defines an accessible logical dimension from the symplectic rank of local logical classes, and it prices Tanner, Steiner, and scheduling costs of laying the code on a network. On four CSS qLDPC codes the authors check duality and monotonicity. For those codes, a bivariate-bicycle example has the sharpest access transition, and embedding costs vary by more than an order of magnitude across topologies.
