CORE: Correlated decoding reweights one decoding subgraph based on the other's correction. Under circuit-level noise, standard reweighting can make matching and clustering decoders fail on (d-1)/2-size fault sets that a distance-d surface code should correct, reducing suppression deep below threshold. A regularized reweighting rule restores the nominal distance scaling.
