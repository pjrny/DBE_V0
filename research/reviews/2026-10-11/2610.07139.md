CORE: A continuous-variable flag is an auxiliary oscillator whose phase-space position records the continuous error that an ancilla decay dumps into a storage mode during a qubit-oscillator gate. Heterodyne measurement of the flag, then feedback, undoes that error up to a bosonic stabilizer. Simulations with circuit-QED parameters are reported to cut logical errors by more than three orders of magnitude for the parity measurement of a four-component cat code, and to put a finite-energy GKP logical lifetime near the noiseless-ancilla bound.
