CORE: Two explicit families of binary CSS qLDPC codes with check weight six are given. The first uses a fixed circulant shift. Its parameters are written as code{6N}{2N+2}{6}, with Tanner-graph girth twelve for N=31 and every N at least 37. A uniform weight-six logical operator is used to fix the distance. The second uses fixed matrix polynomials with circulant and affine-permutation coefficients, and a commutation identity that keeps orthogonality for every cyclic lift. Four realizations are [[192, 16, 8]], [[288, 16, 12]], [[384, 16, 14]], and [[576, 32, 20]], with Tanner girth eight and computationally certified distances. The paper separates classical cycle distance from quantum distance and states limitations of both families.
