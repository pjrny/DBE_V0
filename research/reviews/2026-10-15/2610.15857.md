CORE: Bucket reinforcement-learned sequential scheduling quantizes learned Q-values into integer priorities and updates variable nodes of a belief-propagation decoder from first-in, first-out buckets. It does not retrain the policy or change the messages. A fixed number of priority levels makes variable-node selection and queue moves constant time. On the [[288, 12, 18]] bivariate bicycle code, depolarizing noise at p=0.05, eight priority levels give approximately one fifth the block error rate of flooding belief propagation followed by order-10 ordered-statistics decoding. The cap is 1000 sequential iterations for the bucket scheduler and 1000 flooding iterations for the belief-propagation stage of the reference.
