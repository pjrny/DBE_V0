CORE: The paper studies phase diffusion and entrainment of a time-crystalline oscillation in a thermal-vapor Rydberg ensemble. A pulsed rf heterodyne drive entrains the frequency while it is on. While it is off, the entrained phase diffuses on a 1/e timescale up to 2.3 ms, which the abstract says is significantly longer than the Rydberg state lifetime. The entrainment and diffusion times depend on the strength and detuning of the rf field. A classical Fokker-Planck model with an Adler driving term reproduces the measurements. The abstract points to uses that need frequency and phase stability.
