CORE: DAWG runs an ensemble of serial min-sum belief-propagation decoders that move posteriors from the full detector graph onto sparsified graphs, each with its own transfer matrix. On bivariate bicycle codes and superdense color-code circuits, DAWG plus an order-0 localized-statistics post-processor matches Relay belief-propagation and Vibe in the authors' comparisons, while calling that post-processor about ten times less often than Vibe at the same ensemble size. The post-processor then runs on a graph that is only a fraction of the full detector model.
