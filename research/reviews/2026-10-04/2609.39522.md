CORE: Layer codes are three-dimensional local CSS codes with check weight at most six, made by quasi-concatenating an input code with surface-code patches. This paper builds the syndrome circuits, including the junctions a borrowed surface-code schedule cannot handle, and compares eighteen of them with surface codes of the same distance under circuit-level noise.
