MATH: Learned sequential belief propagation for a fixed number of iterations, then quaternary bit-flip candidates if the syndrome is not met. Each candidate continues learned sequential BP from the same state, and the continuations can run in parallel. Simulations on representative qLDPC codes over the depolarizing channel are said to improve reliability of learned sequential BP.
