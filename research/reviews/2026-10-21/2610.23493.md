CORE: A symmetry-reduced class of Vlasov-Maxwell equilibria with pressure gradients is solved as a nonlinear boundary-value problem whose discretization keeps an energy invariant exactly. For any distribution of the constants of motion in this reduction, the E×B flow cancels an electrostatic part of the pressure-gradient flow. The setting is deliberately simple and is meant as a starting point for kinetic stability studies, not as a reactor rate.
