CORE: NeST lays silicon spin qubits on nested conveyor-belt shuttling tracks whose motions copy multiplication in a family of groups, so long-range qLDPC checks can be collision-free train moves. A search in that family returns rate-20% codes up to [[1400,280,≤28]]. Circuit-level simulations that include shuttling dephasing give thresholds above 10^{-3} and stabiliser cycles under 50 microseconds. An extrapolation to a logical error of 10^{-14} per qubit per round assumes a ten-fold improvement on early shuttling demonstrations and other physical errors at 0.1%; that extrapolation is not adopted.
