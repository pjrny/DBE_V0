CORE: Beyond Blossom is a CPU MWPM decoder for rotated surface codes. Local predecoding, architecture-aware optimization, and pipeline parallelism are said to cut the matching workload while preserving MWPM optimality. Across distances 9 to 33 and circuit-level physical error rates 0.05% to 0.3%, one CPU core is reported to give a 4.23 times geometric-mean speedup and up to 5.58 times over Sparse Blossom in PyMatching. At distance 33 and physical error rate 0.1%, eight cores decode a block of 33 rounds in 21.40 microseconds on average, 0.65 microseconds per round.
