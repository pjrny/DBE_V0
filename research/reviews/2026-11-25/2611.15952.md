CORE: A qudit generalization of the lifted-product construction over cyclic group algebras extends hypergraph-product and lifted-product codes to prime-power dimensions and yields quasi-cyclic qudit CSS codes. Encoding rates are bounded through additive codes. In the fully stacked case, codes built from lifted Tanner codes on expander graphs are claimed to have Theta(log N) logical qudits and almost linear distance Theta(N/log N). An additive decoder and a linear merged-syndrome decoder are studied. Monte Carlo simulations under a depolarizing channel are said to give lower frame error rates than comparable qubit codes.
