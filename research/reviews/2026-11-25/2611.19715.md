CORE: Proxima schedules syndrome-extraction circuits for CSS qLDPC codes using a detector-error-model metric called proximity, the minimum number of extra faults that turn a seed fault into an undetectable logical failure. It ranks schedules at a target depth on small fault subsets and enlarges the subset only to break ties. Across the tested families the abstract reports low depth, high circuit-level distance bounds, logical error rates 1.4 to 2.6 times lower than the strongest automated baseline and up to 40 times lower than others. Using a monomial representation it reports depth-8 uniform schedules for bivariate-bicycle codes that beat a depth-7 reference schedule by 2.1 to 5.1 times in the authors' comparison.
