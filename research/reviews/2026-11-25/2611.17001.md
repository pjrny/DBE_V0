CORE: A sign Q of the electron reflection at zero energy is shown to count reflectionless states of a finite Majorana wire: Q = -1 when an odd number of them lie in the lower half of the complex energy plane. In a finite wire that means the two Majorana modes are split by less than their coupling to the contacts. With equal contacts, every sign change of Q, including the bulk topological transition, is an exceptional point. At zero temperature, Q = -1 exactly when the zero-bias conductance at a contact exceeds e^2/h. The abstract says ordinary bound states mimic the zero-bias peak and that whether Majorana modes have been observed remains debated.
