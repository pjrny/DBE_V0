CORE: Multibeam stimulated Brillouin scattering is studied when one side-scattered wave draws energy from several pumps, each through a separate ion-acoustic branch. The scattered waves can share a wave-vector region and still be distinct optical modes because their frequencies differ. Local-growth and finite-width-gain models, three-dimensional coupled-mode calculations, and two-dimensional PIC simulations are reported. Adding the extra branches increases temporal growth and spatial gain across the tested crossing angles. At fixed total power the coupled response stays strong as the power per beam falls, while the direct-only response drops. PIC spectra show nearby scattered-light features with several pump-specific ion-acoustic branches.
