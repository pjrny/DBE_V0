CORE: Quotient codes, including balanced-product, bivariate-bicycle, genon, and the five-qubit code, are identified with twisted compactification. For monomial quotients of Pauli stabilizer codes a transfer theorem gives logical qudits, operators, and distance bounds from the parent. Higher-form twists can turn abelian Pauli codes into non-abelian order such as D(D4). Twists also give logical gates, including a logical CCZ from three toric codes on RP^3 and, for a quarter-turn twist of the 3D fermionic toric code, codes of linear distance from [[768,2,9]] up whose logical action is identified as T up to Clifford factors. Twisting abelian gauge theories is said to reach only the quantum doubles D(G) whose commutator subgroup is central.
