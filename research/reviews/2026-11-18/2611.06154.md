CORE: Local weak measurements are treated as a Bayesian inference problem for topological stabilizer codes and mapped to classical spin models with generalized symmetries. When the wavefunction deformation and the Gaussian measurement strength coincide, an enlarged symmetry gives identities among correlators that measure whether nonlocal observables stay learnable. The relations are shown for the rank-2 toric code and the X-cube model, and extended to finite-group quantum doubles, including non-Abelian ones, where anyon representation and fusion rules add constraints under positivity conditions.
