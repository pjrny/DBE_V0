CORE: A singlet fabric is proposed for bivariate-bicycle codes on a silicon node network. Singlets generated on shuttling lanes are consumed locally to measure nonlocal stabilizers. Two extraction circuits, distributed GHZ states and ancilla teleportation, are compared with CAbLECAR, which moves an ancilla along the check. On the authors' node lattice the mean scheduled route for the [[144,12,12]] Gross code drops from 350 to 153 micrometers, and the logical error rate is lower by up to 50-fold in the sampled bit-flip regime. Direct transport then beats either singlet circuit at every sampled Gross-code point. With frequent transport bit flips, the GHZ circuit overtakes direct transport once two-qubit gate and readout errors fall below about 4 to 5 times 10^-4, about half the value assumed in the paper.
