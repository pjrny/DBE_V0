CORE: Completion qubits are introduced so that checks produced by gauging a Clifford Z2 symmetry of a qubit stabilizer code commute, including outside the code space. One completion qubit per noncommuting pair is said to preserve the gauged code space and its distance, keep the checks Clifford, and preserve the LDPC property at constant-factor qubit overhead. For qubit codes, gauging and ungauging a distance-d parent is claimed to implement a logical measurement with circuit fault distance Omega(d) in O(d) rounds. The construction is demonstrated by gauging two toric codes into the D(D4) quantum double and back, with an explicit circuit whose fault distance equals the parent distance.
