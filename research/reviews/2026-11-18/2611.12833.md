CORE: GARI++ is a two-stage decoder for surface, bivariate-bicycle, and color codes. The first stage is normalized min-sum with random serial scheduling on a transformed matrix. The second stage runs only when the first does not converge. Soft information and the residual syndrome are passed forward. Under uniform circuit-level depolarizing noise at physical error rate 0.001, the abstract reports FPGA latencies per round from 201 ns to 11.8 microseconds for surface-code distances 7 to 25, and average residual decoding below one microsecond on one Apple M2 core for surface-code distances up to 51 with Union-Find, at accuracy on par with correlated MWPM.
